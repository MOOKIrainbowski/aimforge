Drills

Four drills, one range

Each mode trains a distinct aiming skill, with difficulty presets that scale target size, speed, and wave size together.

01 ◎

Gridshot

Flick to one target at a time. Tracks flick overshoot/undershoot bias and time-to-kill.

02 〜

Tracking

Hold your crosshair on a strafing target. Scored by continuous time-on-target.

03 ⤨

Target Switching

Clear waves of simultaneous targets, training fast re-acquisition between them.

04 ⚡

Reaction Time

Targets flash briefly and vanish. Measures raw reaction speed and false starts.
